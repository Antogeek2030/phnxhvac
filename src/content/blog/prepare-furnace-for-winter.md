Cold snaps arrive quickly in Chester County. A little preparation goes a long way toward avoiding no-heat emergencies.

## Change or Clean the Filter

A dirty filter restricts airflow, reduces efficiency, and can cause the furnace to overheat and shut down. Check monthly during heating season.

## Schedule a Professional Tune-Up

A fall maintenance visit includes safety checks on the heat exchanger, ignitor, flame sensor, and gas connections—plus cleaning and adjustments that improve reliability.

## Test the Thermostat

Set the system to heat and confirm it responds. Replace batteries in programmable or smart thermostats so they do not fail on the coldest night.

## Clear the Area Around the Furnace

Keep storage, boxes, and flammable materials away from the unit and ensure return air pathways are unobstructed.

## Know the Warning Signs

Unusual smells, pilot or ignition failures, and short cycling are reasons to call a technician before a full breakdown.
