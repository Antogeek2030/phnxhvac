When a major component fails, the repair-versus-replace decision can feel overwhelming. Here is a straightforward framework we use with customers.

## Age of the Equipment

Most central AC and furnace systems last 12–15 years with good maintenance. Beyond that, efficiency drops and failures become more frequent.

## Cost of the Repair

A common rule of thumb: if the repair approaches or exceeds 50% of the cost of a new system, replacement often makes better long-term sense—especially on older units.

## Frequency of Recent Repairs

Multiple service calls in the last two seasons usually signal declining reliability. Investing in a new, properly sized system can reduce headaches and energy costs.

## Comfort and Efficiency Goals

Newer systems offer better humidity control, quieter operation, and significantly higher SEER and AFUE ratings. If comfort has been an ongoing complaint, replacement may solve more than just the current failure.
