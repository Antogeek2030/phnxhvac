Pennsylvania summers put serious demand on air conditioning systems. Catching problems early saves money and keeps your home comfortable.

## 1. Weak or Uneven Cooling

If some rooms stay warm while others cool fine, you may have airflow issues, a failing blower, or low refrigerant. A technician can measure temperatures and pressures to pinpoint the cause.

## 2. Strange Noises

Grinding, screeching, or banging usually means a motor bearing, loose part, or failing compressor. Ignoring noise often leads to a more expensive failure.

## 3. Higher Energy Bills

A sudden jump in electricity use with no change in habits often points to a system working harder than it should—dirty coils, low refrigerant, or a struggling compressor.

## 4. Frequent Cycling

Short on/off cycles wear out components and never properly dehumidify the air. Thermostat, sizing, or control problems are common culprits.

## 5. Water or Ice Around the Unit

Clogged condensate drains or freezing evaporator coils need prompt attention to prevent water damage and further system stress.
